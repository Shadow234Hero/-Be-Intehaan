Priji

1st August

My dearest Priji,

I don't know if a website can hold everything I feel, but I wanted to try anyway. Girlfriend's Day felt like the perfect excuse to finally put into words what I usually just feel and never quite say — so here it is, in writing, so you can come back to it whenever you need reminding.

Thank you for being patient with me, for laughing at things that weren't even that funny, for showing up in the small ordinary moments as much as the big ones. You make being known feel safe, and that is not a small thing to give someone.

I hope today you feel exactly as loved as you make me feel every other day of the year. This is just the beginning of the things I want to build with you, page by page.

Always yours,
your priji
